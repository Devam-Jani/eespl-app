## What this role is for

You see the whole company: the numbers, what is late, what waits for your approval, and who did what. You approve what is above the limits and release held payments, and only you can send a quotation or tender past its checklist.

Your role is ticked for two-step sign-in. You can read every role's SOP (**SOP for**, at the top of How to).

## Handoffs

**Approvals.** A PO above the limit (store and purchase) → you approve it. A held vendor payment (accounts) → you release or override it. A quotation or tender stopped by the send checklist (sales, estimator) → only you can send it past, with a logged reason. Extra items and variations (billing) → you approve them.

**Escalations.** Every overdue step in the process rules comes to you after its time.

## Common mistakes

- An override or a release without a full reason is refused, or does nothing (see the bugs list). Write a full reason.
- The tiles show the period picked at the top: change it before you compare.

## Quick card

The things you do every day:

1. Open the **Dashboard**: the red tiles first.
2. **My work**: the approvals waiting for you.
3. **Escalated**: what is overdue on any site.
4. Approve POs above the limit; release held payments.
5. Look at the site profit and the bills' ageing every week.
6. Read the weekly reports.
7. Check the audit log for anything unusual.
