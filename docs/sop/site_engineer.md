## What this role is for

You run your sites. You make sure each day's work is reported, checked and measured, so that it can be billed. You ask for the material your sites need and follow it until it arrives, and you raise and close the snags and issues.

You see only the sites you are a member of. You do not see costs, margins, salaries, vendor bills or the company's accounts.

## Handoffs

**Daily report.** Planning's schedule → the site supervisor writes the day's report → you check it and approve it, or return it with a reason, on the site's daily report tab → the progress, the ready-to-bill list, the client portal and planning.

**Material.** You ask for material (**Ask for material** on the phone, or Material requests) → planning approves it against the estimate → it becomes an indent → store and purchase make the PO → the delivery note → the site counts and confirms it (Deliveries at site) → the GRN.

**Snags and issues.** The client or you raise a snag → you assign it and fix it, with an "after" photo → the client verifies it in the portal.

**Measurement.** Your approved work → billing's measurement book → the RA bill.

## Common mistakes

- "Write the work done before submitting": the report has no "Work done today" text. On the phone this happens on the last place of the day, because the phone screen has no box for it (see the bugs list). Type the text on the site's daily report tab, then send.
- "A DPR cannot be written for a future day": the phone's date is wrong.
- "<qty> <unit> is more than the estimate has left (<left> <unit>): say why": fill **Why more than the estimate** before you send.
- "No material estimate for this site yet": planning makes the estimate before anyone can ask for material.
- "Attach an 'after' photo before marking it fixed": add the after photo first.
- "Attendance cannot be marked for a future day": check the date.

## Quick card

The things you do every day:

1. Open **My work** first: everything waiting for you, the most urgent first.
2. **My day (check-in)**: start the day, and check in at the site with the selfie.
3. Check and approve the supervisor's daily report on the site's daily report tab.
4. Mark the attendance.
5. Ask for material in time, with the reason when it is above the estimate.
6. Count each delivery when it arrives and confirm it under **Deliveries at site**.
7. Close snags with an "after" photo; raise issues as soon as you see them.
8. End the day in **My day**: your travel claim's km come from it.
