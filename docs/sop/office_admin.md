## What this role is for

You keep the office running in the app: the company's settings, the masters (clients, vendors, products), the help texts, and day-to-day help for the people using it.

Your role is ticked for two-step sign-in. You can manage users only within your own rights. With today's permissions, that is the Estimator, Site receiver and Office admin roles only (see the bugs list); the super admin adds the others.

## Handoffs

**People.** A new joiner → the super admin creates the login (you can for the roles you may give) → you add them to the site teams → they get the set-password link.

**Masters.** Sales, purchase and billing ask for a new client, vendor or product → you add it.

## Common mistakes

- Giving or changing a role you do not hold yourself is refused: ask the super admin.
- "Give 3 to 6 steps": a help text needs 3 to 6 steps.

## Quick card

The things you do every day:

1. Open **My work** and **Escalated**.
2. Help people with logins, unlocks and site teams.
3. Keep the clients, vendors and products current.
4. Keep the company settings and process rules current.
5. Edit a page's help text when a step changes.
