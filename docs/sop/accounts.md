## Handoffs

**Client money.** Billing's certified bill → the invoice → the payment follow-up (calls logged) → you enter the receipt → the ageing and the site's profit.

**Vendor money.** Store and purchase's approved GRN → the vendor bill, matched to the PO and the GRN → approval → the payment (the director releases a held one) → Tally.

**Wages.** The site's attendance → payroll for the month → you lock and pay it.

## Common mistakes

- "Bill GRNs, or give the lines of a direct bill": a material bill needs at least one ticked GRN.
- "... 's bill ... is already entered": this vendor bill number is already in the app.
- Pressing **Cancel** on "Accept the differences flagged on this bill?" leaves the bill as it was. Accept only when you mean it.
- **Release** needs a reason of at least 3 characters and **Override** a note of at least 5; a shorter one shows "The reason is too short". Write a full reason.
- "... is locked (paid)": the payroll month is paid and locked.

## Quick card

The things you do every day:

1. Open **My work**: payments due and bills to approve.
2. Log the follow-up calls on overdue bills, and enter the receipts.
3. Enter the vendor bills and match them to the PO and GRN.
4. Pay the approved bills.
5. Approve the petty cash entries.
6. Run payroll at the month's end, and lock it when paid.
7. Export the day book to Tally.
