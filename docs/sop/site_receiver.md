## What this role is for

You are the applicator's person on a site. Your one job in the app is to count each delivery of our material when it reaches the site, and to confirm it.

You see only the deliveries for the sites you are a member of. You see no prices, no costs and no other pages.

## Handoffs

**Delivery.** Store and purchase send the material with a delivery note → you count it and confirm the quantities, with the reason for any shortage or damage → the site store's stock; store and purchase get the difference; accounts check the vendor's bill against your count.

## Common mistakes

- "This receipt link has expired: ask the store for a new delivery note": ask store and purchase for a new note.
- "Already confirmed by <name> at <date time>": someone has already counted this delivery.
- "This receipt link is not valid": the link is wrong, or a newer note replaced it. Use the newest note.

## Quick card

The things you do every day:

1. Open **My work**: the deliveries waiting for your count.
2. Count every item when the truck arrives, before it leaves.
3. Confirm the delivery with the counted quantities; give the reason for any shortage or damage.
4. Photograph damaged material.
5. Tell the site engineer when a delivery has not come.
