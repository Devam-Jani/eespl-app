## What this role is for

You turn measured work into bills. You lock the work order's units and rates, keep the measurement book, check that every finished stage is billed, and make the RA bills and follow them through the client.

You do not see salaries or vendor payments.

## Handoffs

**Work order.** Sales bring the client's work order → you lock the units and rates.

**Measurement to bill.** Approved site work → you measure it in the measurement book → the client approves the set in the portal → **Ready to bill** and the billing checklist → you make the RA bill under Client billing and submit it → the client certifies it → accounts make the invoice and enter the payment; sales follow it up.

**Labour.** The site's labour claims → you check them (**Labour bill check**) → accounts pay them.

## Common mistakes

- **Raise RA bill** makes the bill, then opens a page that does not exist, and you land on the home page (see the bugs list). Find the new bill under **Client billing**.
- "Finish (submit or cancel) the draft RA bill first": a draft is already open for the site.
- "This site has no client contract yet": make the contract first, from the won tender.
- "'<item>': n sqm is beyond the BOQ qty m; bill the excess as an extra item": lower the quantity and bill the excess as an **Extra item**.
- "This place is outside our RFI zones (...): give the reason to measure it": give the reason.
- **Cancel bill** leaves that bill's measurements tied to it (see the bugs list). Tell the office admin before billing them again.

## Quick card

The things you do every day:

1. Open **My work**: bills and measurements waiting for you.
2. Measure approved work into the measurement book, in the work order's units.
3. Send the measurement sets to the client to approve.
4. Check **Ready to bill** and the billing checklist.
5. Make and submit the RA bills.
6. Follow each bill until the client certifies it.
7. Check the labour bills.
