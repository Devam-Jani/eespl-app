You do not see salaries or the company's accounts.

## Handoffs

**Indent to stock.** The site or planning raises the indent (an approved material request makes one) → you approve it → an RFQ to vendors (optional) → the PO (above the limit, the director or office admin approves it) → mark it sent → the delivery note → the site counts the delivery, or you receive it at the godown → the GRN → stock → accounts get the vendor bill.

**Transfer.** Godown to site → the delivery note → the site confirms the count.

## Common mistakes

- "<item>: above agreed rate by n% (contract ₹x per unit): give the reason": the PO rate is above the rate contract.
- "<PO code> is <status>; material is received against an approved PO": the PO is not approved yet, or is closed.
- "<item>: n unit accepted but only m pending on <PO code>": you are accepting more than is still due. Put the extra under **Rejected**.
- **Mark sent** does not e-mail the vendor: send the PO yourself.

## Quick card

The things you do every day:

1. Open **My work**: indents to approve and POs to place.
2. Approve indents; make RFQs or POs from them.
3. Send the POs (with approval above the limit).
4. Receive at the godown: the GRN, with rejections and reasons.
5. Dispatch transfers to sites with the delivery note.
6. Follow the sites' delivery confirmations and differences.
7. Keep the rate contracts and vendors current.
