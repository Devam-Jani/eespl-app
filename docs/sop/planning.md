## What this role is for

You turn won work into sites: the scope, the stages, the areas and the team. You make the material estimate and approve the material the sites ask for, and you make sure every site is measured and billed.

You see all sites. You do not see salaries, vendor bills or the company's accounts.

## Handoffs

**Enquiry.** A new enquiry (from Kylas or typed in) → you allocate it to a salesperson → sales.

**Won tender to site.** Sales and the estimator win the tender → the site is made from it → you set the stages from a template, the towers and floors, the areas and the team → the site engineer and supervisor work on it.

**Material.** The site asks for material → you approve or reject it against the estimate → the approved request becomes an indent for store and purchase.

**New area.** The site finds an area that is not in the scope → you approve it → billing.

## Common mistakes

- "Put these measured areas in the estimate or exclude them with a reason first: ...": add the missed areas, or exclude them with a reason, before you issue the estimate.
- "<code> is issued: make a new one to change it": an issued estimate is frozen.
- "<material> has no coverage in the library: planning adds it first": add the coverage in the Material library.
- Stage templates and Checklist templates are in your menu, but your role cannot edit them (see the bugs list). Ask the office admin.

## Quick card

The things you do every day:

1. Open **My work** and the **Site status board**: what is late, and what waits for you.
2. Allocate the new enquiries to sales.
3. Set up new sites: stages, areas and team.
4. Decide the material requests against the estimate.
5. Approve or reject the new areas found on site.
6. Check the billing checklist: every finished stage measured and billed.
7. Look at **Escalated** for anything overdue on any site.
