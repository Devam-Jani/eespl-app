## What this role is for

You price the work: the BOQs and rates of tenders and quotations, from the rate library, the products and the systems. You keep that library right.

You do not see salaries, vendor bills or the company's accounts.

## Handoffs

**Tender.** Sales bring the enquiry and the client's BOQ → you import the BOQ and match the items to the library → you price them (rates, margin) → the send checklist → sales send it → won → billing locks the units and rates in the work order.

**Library.** Purchase rates and rate contracts from store and purchase → products and systems → your rates.

## Common mistakes

- The send checklist stops a tender below the margin floor or without its terms: fix the rates or the terms. Only the director can send past it, with a reason.
- **Mark final** on a rate working needs `knowledge.edit`: ask the office admin if it is refused.
- A BOQ line with no match in the library stays unpriced: match it, or price it by hand.

## Quick card

The things you do every day:

1. Open **My work**: tenders and quotations waiting for prices.
2. Import the client's BOQ and match each line to the library.
3. Price the lines and check the margin.
4. Clear the tender's checks and terms.
5. Keep the rate library, products and systems current.
6. Hand the priced tender to sales to send.
