## What this role is for

You are the client's person for our work, in the client portal. You see the progress of your own sites. You approve measurements, raise and verify snags and comment, as far as your client role allows.

You see only your own sites. You see none of our costs.

## Handoffs

**Measurements.** Our billing team sends a set of measurements → you approve it, or correct a line → the RA bill.

**Snags.** You raise a snag with a photo → our site engineer fixes it, with an "after" photo → you verify it, or reopen it.

## Common mistakes

- "Your client role does not allow this (...)": the portal shows a button your client role may not use (see the bugs list). Ask your company's portal admin or our office.
- An invite link lasts 7 days by default: ask us for a new one when it has expired.

## Quick card

The things you do every day:

1. Open your site from the portal home.
2. Look at the progress and the latest photos.
3. Approve the measurement sets sent to you.
4. Raise snags with a photo.
5. Verify fixed snags, or reopen them.
