Enquiries that came to planning directly, without a salesperson. Allocate each to a salesperson;
they get it on their to-do at once. The list shows every open lead with no owner (not won, lost or
junk), oldest first.

**On the page**
- **New enquiry**: opens the **New enquiry** form.
- **Enquiry**: the contact name and lead code, with company, city and phone under it.
- **Requirement**: what the client asked for.
- **Waiting**: days since the enquiry came in.
- **Salesperson** (drop-down): the people with the sales role. **Choose…** until one is picked.
- **Allocate**: enabled once a salesperson is picked. The enquiry leaves this list.
- A row opened from a notification is highlighted.

**New enquiry**
- **Contact name \*** (required), **Phone**, **Company**, **City**, **Requirement**.
- **Save to the queue**: makes a lead with status New and no owner, notes "Enquiry received by planning (not allocated)" on its timeline, and puts it in this list.
- **Cancel**: closes without saving.

**What happens next**
- Saving a new enquiry tells planning "Enquiry ...: ..., .... Allocate a salesperson." If nobody allocates it in 24 hours, the director is told.
- **Allocate** makes the salesperson the lead's owner, notes "Allocated to ... by planning" on the timeline, and tells the salesperson "Enquiry ... allocated to you: ... Prepare the quotation." If no quotation is made from the lead in 3 days, planning is reminded.

**Rules**
- Only an active user can be picked.
- The salesperson then works the lead on Leads; the lead stays visible to planning there.

**Hands off**
- Receives: phone calls and walk-ins that reach planning; enquiries that came in without a salesperson.
- Passes on: the lead to the salesperson.

**When it goes wrong**
- "Enquiry not found": it was removed; reload the page.
- "Pick an active salesperson": the person left or was switched off; pick another.
- "No enquiry waiting." means every enquiry has an owner.
