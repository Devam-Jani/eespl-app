Your own problem reports and where each one stands: the ones you write here, the ones you sent
with "Report this" on the helper card, and the errors the app recorded while you were working. It
is opened from your name at the top right, then **My reports**.

**On the page**
- **Report a problem** (field): what you were doing and what went wrong (up to 2000 characters).
- **Send**: sends it to the issue log. The page shows "Thank you: it is in the issue log as #n."
  and the report appears in the list. **Send** stays grey while the box is empty.
- The list: **#** (the issue number), **When**, **Problem** (the title and the page it happened
  on), **Your words**, **Status**.
- **Status**: **New**, **User mistake, guided**, **Bug**, **Being fixed**, **Fixed** (with what
  was fixed) or **Will not fix**. "Live since <date>" shows when the fix is in the app.
- "You have not reported anything. ..." when the list is empty.

**Rules**
- You see only your own reports, never anyone else's.
- The same error reported by several people is one issue in the log; each person's report is kept
  with it.
- Write what you clicked, on which page, and what you expected. Do not type passwords.

**What happens next**
- The director, the office admin and the super admin read the issue log (Settings > Issue log),
  set the status and write the fix. The status here changes with it.

**Hands off**
- Receives: the status and the fix from the issue log.
- Passes on: your report to the issue log (director, office admin, super admin).

**When it goes wrong**
- "It could not be sent just now. Try again in a minute.": the server did not take it. Wait a
  minute and press **Send** again; your words are cleared, so copy them first if they are long.
