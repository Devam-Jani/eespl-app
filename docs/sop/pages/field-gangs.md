Gang check-in records a labour contractor's gang arriving at and leaving a site, with one group
photo and the phone's location. It gives who was there, how many and how many hours. It is made for
the phone.

**On the page**
- **Site** (field): the site where the gang is. Only your sites are listed.
- **Labour contractor** (field): the gang, with the number of its workers in brackets. Only
  contractors with active workers on the labour list appear.
- "Tick who is here, or give the headcount:": tick the workers present, or leave them all unticked
  and type the **Headcount**.
- **Group photo**: opens the back camera. It turns into "Group photo taken: retake".
- **Why outside the site circle?**: appears when you are farther from the site's pin than its
  radius. Write the reason and press the check-in button again.
- **Check in n at the site**: takes your location and records the gang in (n is the number ticked
  or typed). It stays grey until the site, the contractor, the photo and the names or the headcount
  are given.
- **Today**: the gangs checked in today on your sites: contractor, site, number of people (with the
  names when ticked), the time in, and once out the time out and "n h each". A check-in outside the
  circle shows the distance and the reason in red.
- **Check the gang out**: takes your location and records the time out. Outside the circle the app
  asks for the reason in a box ("The reason:").

**Rules**
- One photo of the gang is required at check-in.
- Ticked names must all belong to the chosen contractor's gang.
- A gang can be checked in at one site at a time. Check it out before checking it in elsewhere.
- Outside the site's radius (150 m by default) a reason is needed; the distance is kept with the
  record.
- You only work on gangs at the sites you are posted to.

**What happens next**
- The visit (people, hours, photo, location) is kept and shown with the site's visits.
- Mark the workers' attendance on the site's labour tab as usual: the gang check-in does not mark
  it for you.

**Hands off**
- Receives: the contractors and their workers from whoever keeps **Labour**.
- Passes on: the gang's presence and hours at the site to the site engineer and planning.

**When it goes wrong**
- "Location is blocked: allow location for this site in the browser": allow location for the app
  in the browser, then try again.
- "Could not get your location; try again in the open": move outside and try again.
- "Take one photo of the gang": press **Group photo** first.
- "Pick the names or give the headcount": tick the names or type the headcount.
- "Every name must be of this contractor's gang": pick the contractor again and tick only its
  workers.
- "You are n m from <site>'s pin: give the reason": write why in the box. If the site's pin is
  wrong, ask the site engineer to correct it.
- "This gang is checked in at a site: check it out there first": find it under **Today** (or at the
  other site) and press **Check the gang out**.
- "The gang is already checked out": reload the page.
- "Missing permission: field.gang": your role does not check gangs in.
