One picture of the whole job, from the first lead to the guarantee, in ten steps with the role that
does each. Open it from your name at the top right, then **How the app works**. Data moves along the
arrows; nobody types it twice.

**On the page**
- Steps 1 to 5 (left to right): **1. Lead and site visit** (Sales), **2. Quotation** (Sales,
  estimator), **3. Tender and BOQ** (Estimator, from the rate library), **4. Work order** (Billing
  locks units and rates), **5. Site, stages and 3D** (Planning, from the drawing).
- Steps 6 to 10 (right to left, under them): **6. Daily work** (Site engineer: reports,
  materials), **7. Measurement** (Billing; client approves), **8. RA bill** (Billing, accounts),
  **9. Payment** (Accounts, with follow-ups), **10. Certificate and guarantee** (Billing, director
  signs).
- Press a box to open that step's page. A box your role cannot open is shown as a picture only (it
  does nothing when pressed).
- **Shared by every step**: **Rate library and products**, **Users and roles**, **Stores: indent,
  PO, GRN**, **Client portal**, **Issue log and helper**. Each opens its page when your role may
  open it.

**Rules**
- Which boxes open depends only on your role's rights; the picture is the same for everyone.
- For steps with two pages (work order, RA bill, payment), the box opens the first one your role
  may use (for example sales open their own bills).

**What happens next**
- Use it to see where your work comes from and where it goes. Your role's own SOP (Help > How to)
  has the button-by-button steps.

**Hands off**
- Receives: nothing.
- Passes on: nothing; it is for reading.

**When it goes wrong**
- A box does not open: your role does not use that page. Ask the role named in the box.
