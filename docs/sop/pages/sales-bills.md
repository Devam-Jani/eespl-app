Your clients' bills: what was billed, what the client certified, what has come in, and how late the
rest is. It is read only: billing makes and works the bills; to chase a payment, speak to billing.
You see the bills of the sites you are on or in charge of, and of sites made from your own tenders.
Draft and cancelled bills are not shown.

**On the page**
- **Search bills** (box, "Search bill, site or client"): filters the list as you type.
- **Bill no.**: the invoice number; the RA bill code shows under it when it differs. Before the invoice is made, the RA bill code.
- **Date**: the invoice date (or the end of the bill period before invoicing).
- **Client and site**: the client, with the site code and name under it.
- **Amount**: the bill's gross amount.
- **Certified**: what the client certified; "—" until they certify.
- **Received**: the payments received against the invoice.
- **Overdue**: days past the due date while money is still owed, as a red badge; "—" when not overdue.
- "No bills for your clients yet." when there is nothing to show.

**Rules**
- No costs, steps at the client or follow-up notes are shown here.
- Up to the last 300 bills are listed, newest first.

**What happens next**
- The app tells the salesperson when a bill is sent to the client ("follow up for certification"), when the client certifies less than billed ("Give the reason"), when a bill misses the client's billing window or is stuck at a step, and when a payment comes in.
- Follow up with the client, then tell billing what the client said.

**Hands off**
- Receives: bills and payments recorded by billing and accounts.
- Passes on: the client's answers to billing (certification, payment dates).

**When it goes wrong**
- "Missing permission: closeout.payments": your role does not see bills here.
- A bill you expect is missing: you are not on that site, or the bill is still a draft. Ask billing.
