This guide, in the app. It opens at your own role's SOP. The super admin and the director can open every role's SOP; everyone else sees only their own.

**On the page**
- **How to** (top of every page, next to **?**): opens this guide at the section for the page you were on. If the page is not in your role's SOP, the guide opens at the start.
- **How to (my SOP)** (your menu, top right) and "How to: my role's SOP" in search (Ctrl K): open the guide at the start.
- **SOP for** (list): only when you hold more than one role, or you are the super admin or the director. Pick the role whose SOP you want to read. Your own roles are marked "(yours)".
- **Contents**: press a heading to jump to it. The section you came from has a coloured frame.
- In the client portal, **How to** at the top opens the client's SOP.

**Rules**
- A person reads the SOPs of the roles they hold. Opening another role's SOP gives "This SOP belongs to another role".
- The text comes with the app: it changes when the pages change. The **?** steps of a page are the short version, which the office admin can edit in Settings > Help texts.
- The printed copy (PDF and Word, from the office) has the pictures; the in-app guide is text only.
