Billing against cost for every site that is not closed and has any billing or cost so far. It shows what was billed, received and held back as retention, the cost to date, the gross profit and margin, and the margin projected at completion.

**On the page**
- One row per site:
  - **Site**: the site code (click it to open the site on its **Finance** tab, with the full breakdown of billing and of cost by head) and the site name.
  - **Billed**: the issued tax invoices, net of GST, less credit notes.
  - **Received**: the receipts set against the site's invoices, including the client's TDS. Advances not yet set against an invoice are not counted here.
  - **Retention**: retention deducted on the invoices and not yet released.
  - **Cost to date**: material, labour, subcontract, equipment, freight, other costs and petty cash spent on the site.
  - **Gross profit**: billed minus cost to date. Red when it is below zero.
  - **Margin**: gross profit as a share of billed. "—" when nothing is billed yet.
  - **Projected margin**: the contract value minus the budget at completion, as a share of the contract value. "—" when the site has no contract value or no budget.
- A row is highlighted when the cost to date is more than what is billed.

**Rules**
- Only roles that may see costs and margins open this page. The figures are the same as on the site's **Finance** tab.
- Staff salary is counted in the cost of a site only for roles that may see payroll. For other roles the cost leaves it out, so the same site shows a lower cost and a higher profit.
- Your cost figures include the staff salary of the people on the site. {perm: payroll.view}
- The budget at completion is the site budget, with the tender's budget used for any head the site budget does not set.
- Closed sites are not listed. Only the sites your role may see are listed.
- Nothing can be changed on this page.

**What happens next**
- A site in the red (cost more than billed) is a reason to bill the work done, or to look at the cost on the site's **Finance** and **Budget** tabs.

**Hands off**
- Receives: invoices and receipts from billing and accounts; costs from purchase (GRNs), stores, labour and subcontractor bills, expenses and payroll.
- Passes on: the figures to the director for review.

**When it goes wrong**
- "Profit and margin need tender.margin": your role may not see costs and margins. Ask the office admin.
- "No billing or cost yet.": no open site has an invoice or a cost so far.
- A cost looks missing: a GRN, bill or expense counts only once it is approved or entered. Check it on the site's **Finance** tab.
