Places that are not on a site's list, asked for from the phone with a photo. Planning (or the
director, or the office admin) approves each one into the site's structure, or rejects it and moves
the work already booked on it to the right place. Only roles that approve new areas open this page.

**On the page**
- **pending**, **approved** and **rejected** (tabs): the requests waiting for a decision, and the decided ones.
- Each request: the photo, the name, the site, the area type ("area type not given" when empty), the size ("about <n> sqm" or "size not given"), who asked and when, their note, and the decision note once decided.
- **Approve**: opens "Approve: <name>". Only on a pending request.
- **Reject**: opens "Reject: <name>". Only on a pending request.
- "None pending." (or approved, or rejected) when the tab is empty.

**The Approve box**
- **Add it under**: "the site (top level)", or the place it belongs in. It starts at the place the site gave. If the site gave a place, choosing "the site (top level)" still puts it under that place: pick another place instead.
- **Kind of place**: **other**, **terrace**, **toilet**, **kitchen**, **balcony**, **podium**, **lift pit**, **ug tank**, **oh tank**, **retaining wall**, **raft**, **stp**, **swimming pool**, **flat** or **floor**.
- **Approve**: adds the place. **Cancel** closes the box.

**The Reject box**
- **Move the booked work to**: **Choose a place…** (required). The daily report lines and issues booked on the new area move there.
- **Why**: the reason, kept as the decision note.
- **Reject and move**: rejects it. Grey until a place is chosen.

**Rules**
- A request is decided once. After that it only shows in **approved** or **rejected**.
- Approving adds the place to the site's structure with the asked size as its area. When the site has a survey and a size was given, the place is also added to the site's latest survey as an approximate, manual area ("New area approved from site (approximate size)").
- The new place has no BOQ line, contract line or scope yet. Add those yourself, or the work there is not planned, done by step or billed.

**What happens next**
- Approve: the daily report lines and issues booked on the request now point to the new place. You and planning are told "<name> added at <site>: add it to the BOQ / contract and the material plan", with a link to the site.
- Approve or reject: the "Area not in the quotation" to-do (planning and the salesperson, from a daily report line on this area) closes.
- The "<n> new area waiting" count on the site's Overview goes down.
- The person who asked is not notified. They see the place in the site's list (approved) or their lines moved (rejected).

**Hands off**
- Receives: the request from the site engineer or the site supervisor (New area form on the site).
- Passes on: the new place to planning (BOQ / contract and material plan) and to the site team, who book work on it. The salesperson quotes it when the client must pay for it.

**When it goes wrong**
- "Not waiting for a decision": someone decided it first. Open the **approved** or **rejected** tab.
- "Unknown kind of place": pick a kind from the list again.
- "Pick an area of this site to move the booked work to": choose a place in **Move the booked work to**.
