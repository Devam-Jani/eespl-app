Who brought a tender: a salesperson, an applicator partner or a manufacturer route. Not the end
client. The rate library's folders are channels, so a channel's past rates help price its next
tender.

**On the page**
- **Search channels** (box): type and press Enter.
- **Type** (filter): Salesperson, Applicator partner, Manufacturer route, Other; **All types** clears it.
- **⤓ Export Excel**: downloads the channels as filtered.
- **Add channel**: opens **New channel**. {perm: clients.edit}
- The table: **Name** (notes under it), **Type**, **Rate library lines**, **Tenders**, **Status**.
- Click a row to open **Edit ...**. {perm: clients.edit}
- **Previous** / **Next**: page through the list.

**The channel form** {perm: clients.edit}
- **Name \***, **Type**, **Notes**, **Active**.
- **Save**: saves the channel. **Cancel** closes without saving.
- **Delete** (when editing): asks "Delete the channel ...?".

**Rules**
- Channel names are unique.
- A channel used by tenders or rate library lines cannot be deleted; untick **Active** instead.
- Leads and tenders pick the channel in **Channel (who brought it)**.

**Hands off**
- Receives: new channels from planning or the director.
- Passes on: the channel to leads, tenders and the rate library.

**When it goes wrong**
- "A channel named ... already exists": use the existing one.
- "... is used by n tenders and m rate library lines; set it inactive instead".
- "Missing permission: clients.edit": your role may only look.
