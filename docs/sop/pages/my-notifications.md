Your own choice, per process step, of how its notifications reach you: in the app only, in the app
and by email, or in one summary email a day. It is opened from your name's menu at the top
(**My notifications**) or from **My email choices** on Notifications.

**On the page**
- One row per process step: its number, its name and a choice.
- The choice for each step:
  - **App only**: the bell and Notifications only.
  - **App and email**: also an email at once.
  - **Daily summary email (9:00)**: in the app at once, and by email in one summary each morning.
  - "(default)" marks the company's default for that step.
- A change is saved as soon as you pick it; "Saved." shows at the top.

**Rules**
- Defaults: steps that ask you to act come in the app and by email; information-only steps come in
  the app only.
- Escalations always come by email, whatever you choose here.
- Emails are sent only when the company has email switched on, and only if your user has an email
  address.
- The bell and Notifications always show everything; this page changes only the email.

**What happens next**
- New notifications follow your choice from now on. Earlier ones are not sent again.

**Hands off**
- Receives: the steps and their defaults from Settings > Process rules (office admin).
- Passes on: nothing.

**When it goes wrong**
- "<step>: <choice>?": the choice was not accepted. Reload the page and pick again.
- No emails come: email may not be switched on for the company yet, or your user has no email
  address. Ask the office admin.
