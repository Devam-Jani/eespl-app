What each role of the client's people may see and do in the client portal. The ticks are the
rights given when a client person is invited with that role. Every change is in the audit log.

**On the page**
- The grid: one row per right, one column per client role. The rights are:
  - "See the sites, progress, daily reports, photos and the 3D view"
  - "Enter and update the promised handover dates"
  - "Sign inspections, quality checklists and RFIs"
  - "Raise snags"
  - "Approve or correct measurements"
  - "See the bills"
  - "Certify or reject bills"
  - "See payments and what is overdue"
  - "See the shared drawings and documents"
- A tick box: saved at once when you click it. The column is greyed while it saves.
- A shaded tick box: it differs from the default for that role.
- **Reset to default** (under each column): puts the role back to its default rights. It is greyed when the role already has its defaults.

**Rules**
- Only roles with the client roles right change this grid.
- A change applies to new invites. People already invited keep the rights they were given.

**What happens next**
- The next client person invited with that role gets the new rights in the portal.

**Hands off**
- Receives: what the director agrees each client role may do.
- Passes on: the rights the site team gives when inviting a client person.

**When it goes wrong**
- "Unknown rights: …" or "Client role not found": reload the page and try again.
