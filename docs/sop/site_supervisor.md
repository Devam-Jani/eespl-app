## What this role is for

You run the day on site: who came, what was done, what arrived and what is wrong. Your daily report is the base of the site's progress and of its bills.

You see only the sites you are a member of. You do not see costs, margins, salaries or the company's accounts.

## Handoffs

**Daily report.** Planning's schedule gives you the day's places and steps → you write and send the report → the site engineer approves it, or returns it with a reason → the approved report feeds the progress and billing.

**Material.** You ask for material → planning approves it → indent → PO → delivery note → you count the delivery and confirm it.

**People.** You check in the gangs and mark the attendance → the site engineer, and payroll for the wages.

## Common mistakes

- "Write the work done before submitting": the report has no "Work done today" text. On the phone this happens on the last place of the day. Type the text on the site's daily report tab.
- A report saved on the phone says "Saved on the phone; it sends when the signal is back". If it never clears, the server refused it, and the app does not show why (see the bugs list). Tell the site engineer.
- "Post the worker to one of your sites": **Add worker** has no site field for you (see the bugs list). Ask the site engineer to add the worker.
- "<worker> is already marked at <site code> on <date>": the worker is already marked at another site that day.
- "Attendance cannot be marked for a future day": check the phone's date.

## Quick card

The things you do every day:

1. Open **My work** first: what is due today.
2. Start **My day** and check in at the site.
3. Check in the gangs (**Gang check-in**) and mark the attendance.
4. Write the daily report for each place and step, with photos.
5. Count each delivery and confirm it, with any shortage.
6. Raise snags and issues with a photo as soon as you see them.
7. Ask for material early, with the reason when it is above the estimate.
8. End the day in **My day**.
